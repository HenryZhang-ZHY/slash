# Slash Logo Design

## Monolithic Precision

Monolithic Precision reduces identity to one decisive geometric act. Form is
solid, unornamented, and immediately legible: a single forward slash drawn as
a balanced parallelogram. Negative space is not leftover area but a measured
part of the mark, calibrated with painstaking attention so the symbol remains
clear from favicon scale to large-format display.

Color is absolute rather than decorative. The light-mode mark is pure black on
transparency; the dark-mode mark is pure white on transparency. This binary
palette gives the work the material honesty of ink and cut paper while keeping
it independent of any surrounding interface. The restraint must feel
meticulously crafted, not merely minimal.

Scale and rhythm come from the slash itself. Its long diagonal creates forward
motion, while horizontal terminals hold the shape in equilibrium. The width,
angle, and margins are tuned as if through countless refinements by a
master-level identity designer; no secondary shape competes with that rhythm.

Composition is centered optically within a square `256 × 256` view box. The
mark occupies enough area to feel assured without crowding the edges. Every
coordinate is intentional, producing a silhouette that appears painstakingly
resolved and equally stable in browser chrome, GitHub avatars, documentation,
and product interfaces.

Text is absent from the artwork. Meaning lives entirely in geometry, contrast,
and space. The resulting pair is an original Slash identity informed by the
reference's stark monochrome confidence, while using the product's own `/`
character as its visual DNA.

## Deliverables

- `assets/logo-dark.svg`: transparent background with a white slash.
- Both files use `viewBox="0 0 256 256"`, `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid meet"`,
  a single polygon, and `<title>Slash</title>`.
- Both files have identical geometry: `148,20 198,20 108,236 58,236`.
